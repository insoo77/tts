Julian S. Huxley: Man as a Relative Being

상대적 존재로서의 인간 (相対的存在としての人間)

English

During the present century we have heard so much of the revolutionary discoveries of modern physics that we are apt to forget how great has been the change in the outlook due to biology. Yet in some respects this has been the more important. For it is affecting the way we think and act in our everyday existence. Without the discoveries and ideas of Darwin and the other great pioneers in the biological field, from Mendel to Freud, we should all be different from what we are.
